Use cases

What TRINETRA does in ten real situations.

Each case below is a scripted situation run through the working engine. The chart, the confidence, the explanation, the corrected value and the repair advice are the engine's own output for that situation.

Maintenance prediction

Warn before a sensor fails.

Three stations were given slow sensor ageing with no other fault. The engine measures each sensor's drift against its neighbours and estimates when it will leave tolerance.

The grand challenge

Can AI build a self-aware, self-healing observation network?

Self-aware

Every sensor has a health score, a drift rate and a days-to-tolerance estimate. Every alert has a probability and a plain-language reason.

Self-healing

Faulty readings are replaced by an estimate from neighbours or history. A neighbour that is itself faulty is left out of other stations' checks, so one fault does not spread.

Trustworthy in all conditions

Real heatwaves, storms and fog pass both the physics and neighbour tests and are kept. Stations with no neighbours fall back to physics and AI, with lower confidence.

What is still open

Fully automatic repair needs a technician or a redundant sensor. Real IMD archives still have to confirm the numbers, which were measured on simulated data.
